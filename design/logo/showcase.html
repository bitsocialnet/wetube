Construction

Anatomy of the symbol

  1. 1
    W strokesEach triangle's left and lower edge, read together as \ / \ /. Red #ed004a.
  2. 2
    Top edgesComplete the two play triangles. Pale red #f6a8c0 on light, #6c1636 on dark.
  3. 3
    Bisector splitRed and pale meet on each corner's bisector (dashed), where the border's outer and inner corners line up, so every stroke keeps its full width.
  4. 4
    Square-cut cornersEvery outer corner is clipped by 5 units, echoing the squared details of Chakra Petch.
  5. 5
    Play countersTransparent inner triangles. The second triangle is the first moved right by 62.86 units; the overlap joins them.
